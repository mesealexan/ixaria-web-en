  <h2>2. What Data We Collect</h2>
  <p>We may collect the following types of personal data:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Contact information</strong> — name, email address, company name, phone number when you fill in a form or book a call.</li>
    <li><strong>Usage data</strong> — pages visited, time spent, browser type, referring URL, and IP address, collected through analytics tools.</li>

  </ul>

  <p>We use your personal data to:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Respond to your inquiries and fulfil requests for information or calls.</li>
    <li>Send you the newsletter you subscribed to (you can unsubscribe at any time).</li>
    <li>Improve our website and services through analytics.</li>
    <li>Comply with legal obligations.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>4. Legal Basis for Processing</h2>
  <p>We process your data based on: (a) your consent where applicable; (b) the performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures; (c) our legitimate interests in running and improving our business; and (d) compliance with legal obligations.</p>

  <p>We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted service providers (e.g. email platforms, analytics tools, calendar booking tools) who process it on our behalf under appropriate data processing agreements. We do not transfer data outside the EEA without adequate safeguards.</p>
