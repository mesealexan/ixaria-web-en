<div class="job-section" id="about-ixaria">
<span class="job-section__label">About us</span>
<h2 class="job-section__title">About <span>Ixaria</span></h2>
<p>Ixaria is building a product intelligence system for furniture manufacturers.</p>
<p>
Ixaria helps furniture manufacturers centralize product and component data, understand how customers
interact with their websites, and render better product pages based on customer behavior, needs,
buying stage, and context.
</p>
<p>
We are not building a standard webshop, a simple product configurator, or a collection of
client-specific projects. We are building a scalable platform that integrates with existing
e-commerce systems and combines structured product data, behavioral intelligence, and AI-assisted capabilities.
</p>
<p>
We are a small core team, so this role has a major impact. The person joining Ixaria will help define
how the platform is built, how AI becomes part of the product, how the technical team grows, and
ultimately what the company becomes.
</p>
</div>


<div class="job-section" id="what-you-do">
<span class="job-section__label">Responsibilities</span>
<h2 class="job-section__title">What you will do</h2>
<p>
You will be responsible for the architecture, development, and technical evolution of the Ixaria
platform. This includes:
</p>
<ul class="job-list">
<li>Owning the overall technical architecture and development strategy</li>
<li>Building and improving integrations with Shopify, WooCommerce, and other e-commerce platforms</li>
<li>Developing plugins, applications, APIs, and integration layers for existing webshops</li>
<li>Working across backend systems, frontend applications, databases, infrastructure, and third-party services</li>
<li>Designing the systems used to collect, validate, store, and retrieve structured product and component data</li>
<li>Building the technical foundation for adaptive product page experiences</li>
<li>Developing the event and data infrastructure required to understand customer journeys and website behavior</li>
<li>Ensuring that client implementations become reusable platform capabilities rather than one-off custom solutions</li>
<li>Establishing standards for scalability, performance, security, testing, and maintainable development</li>
<li>Working closely with product, design, analytics, and commercial leadership</li>
<li>Helping define the technical team and development processes as Ixaria grows</li>
</ul>
</div>


<div class="job-section" id="ai">
<span class="job-section__label">AI &amp; LLM</span>
<h2 class="job-section__title">AI and LLM responsibility</h2>
<p>AI and LLM-based capabilities will be an important part of Ixaria's technical direction.</p>
<p>
You will be responsible for defining how these technologies are integrated into the platform in a
reliable, controlled, and commercially useful way. This may include:
</p>
<ul class="job-list job-list--ai">
<li>Building AI-assisted workflows for structuring, classifying, enriching, and validating product data</li>
<li>Using LLMs to identify missing product information and generate suggested customer-facing explanations</li>
<li>Developing retrieval systems that allow AI models to answer questions using approved product and component data</li>
<li>Contributing to the development of an AI product assistant for complex furniture products</li>
<li>Using behavioral and product data to support customer segmentation and product page adaptation</li>
<li>Evaluating when to use third-party models, hosted services, retrieval-augmented generation, or internally controlled systems</li>
<li>Designing validation, approval, monitoring, and fallback mechanisms for AI-generated outputs</li>
<li>Preventing hallucinations and ensuring that sensitive claims remain grounded in manufacturer-approved information</li>
<li>Managing model costs, latency, reliability, data privacy, and observability</li>
<li>Turning AI prototypes into dependable product capabilities</li>
</ul>
<p class="job-note">
We are not looking for someone who simply adds a chatbot to the platform. We need someone who
understands how AI systems should interact with structured data, business rules, user behavior,
and human approval processes.
</p>
</div>
